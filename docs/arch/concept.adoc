== Executive Summary

*Foundation Review* (`foundation-review.nvim`) is a
lightweight Neovim plugin providing AI-assisted proofreading
of prose and markup documents.

Its primary responsibilities are:

* Spelling.
* Grammar.
* Punctuation.
* Detection and suggested rewriting of unwieldy sentences.

The plugin uses a locally hosted language model through
Ollama and presents findings through Neovim's native
quickfix list.

The initial model is `gemma4:12b-it-qat`, subject to
further proofreading-specific evaluation.

All application-specific logic is implemented in Lua.
No custom Swift application, cloud API, external account
or additional application service is required.

The plugin complements conventional language tools,
including Harper LSP, without attempting to replace
their continuous diagnostics.

== Problem Statement

Traditional spelling and grammar tools provide useful
mechanical checks, but some grammatical errors depend
on context and are difficult to detect reliably.

They may also struggle to recognise sentences that are
grammatically valid but unnecessarily long or convoluted.

General-purpose AI assistants can help, but introduce
unnecessary interaction and may suggest changes outside
the author's intended scope.

Foundation Review addresses this problem through a
focused, on-demand proofreading workflow integrated
with Neovim.

== Vision

Provide a local, unobtrusive proofreader that identifies
language errors and difficult sentence construction
without interfering with the author's writing process.

The author invokes a review, examines the findings and
decides whether to make corrections.

The reviewer advises; the author retains control.

== Objectives

The project shall:

. Integrate natively with Neovim using Lua.
. Use local inference through Ollama.
. Operate without external network connectivity.
. Support text and markup document formats.
. Restrict operation to configured filetypes.
. Analyse unsaved buffer contents.
. Support complete-buffer and visual-selection reviews.
. Use British English by default.
. Detect spelling, grammar and punctuation errors.
. Identify unwieldy sentences and suggest restructuring.
. Return structured findings.
. Present findings through native quickfix.
. Preserve accurate source locations.
. Execute asynchronously.
. Never modify document contents automatically.
. Require minimal installation and configuration.

== Scope

=== In Scope

The initial implementation shall support:

* British English spelling checks.
* Context-sensitive grammatical checks.
* Punctuation checks.
* Detection of excessively long or convoluted sentences.
* Suggested rewrites of flagged sentences.
* Filetype-specific protection of non-prose content.
* Document segmentation for model context limits.
* Exact-source finding validation.
* Quickfix navigation.
* Review cancellation.
* Model and service availability checks.

=== Sentence Readability

Sentence readability is a deliberately limited
extension of proofreading.

A long sentence is not necessarily a defective sentence.

The reviewer shall identify sentences whose construction
makes them unnecessarily difficult to understand.

Suggested corrections may:

* Divide a sentence into shorter sentences.
* Simplify unnecessarily complicated syntax.
* Remove structural complexity where meaning is preserved.
* Improve punctuation supporting sentence structure.

The reviewer shall not:

* Flag sentences solely because they exceed a word count.
* Remove factual details.
* Change technical terminology unnecessarily.
* Change the author's intended meaning.
* Rewrite otherwise acceptable prose for stylistic reasons.

A sentence rewrite shall be reported as an advisory
suggestion, not a definite grammatical error.

=== Out of Scope

The initial release shall not include:

* General stylistic editing.
* Document-wide rewriting.
* General clarity or concision analysis outside
  unwieldy sentence construction.
* Ambiguity analysis as an independent review category.
* Redundancy and repetition analysis as independent checks.
* Logical consistency checking.
* Terminology consistency analysis.
* Fact-checking.
* Automatic application of corrections.
* Cloud inference.
* A conversational interface.
* Continuous inference while typing.
* A custom Language Server Protocol implementation.
* Retrieval-augmented generation.
* Document rendering or publishing.

Additional editorial capabilities require an explicit
scope decision and are not implied by the use of an LLM.

== User Experience

=== Normal Workflow

. Open a supported document in Neovim.
. Execute `:FoundationReview`.
. The plugin captures the current buffer contents.
. The document is divided into reviewable sections.
. Each section is submitted to the local Ollama model.
. Structured findings are validated and consolidated.
. The quickfix list is populated and opened.
. The author navigates through the findings.
. Corrections or rewrites are made manually.

=== Proposed Commands

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Command |Purpose

|`:FoundationReview`
|Review the current buffer.

|`:FoundationReviewSelection`
|Review a selected range.

|`:FoundationReviewCancel`
|Cancel the active review.

|`:FoundationReviewHealth`
|Check Ollama availability, configuration and model status.
|===

The command names constitute the proposed public API.

=== Finding Types

The plugin shall distinguish between two finding types.

Correction::
A localised spelling, grammar or punctuation error,
with an exact source quotation and suggested correction.

Sentence rewrite::
An unwieldy sentence, with the complete original
sentence and an advisory replacement.

Both finding types shall use quickfix.

=== Example Quickfix Output

[source,text]
----
article.adoc|24 col 18| W: Grammar: "it's" -> "its"
article.adoc|47 col 1| I: Sentence: Consider splitting sentence
article.adoc|83 col 12| W: Spelling: "organize" -> "organise"
----

Severity classifications:

* `E` -- High-confidence mechanical error.
* `W` -- Likely language error requiring attention.
* `I` -- Advisory sentence rewrite.

Severity shall not be presented as a statistically
calibrated model confidence score.

== Filetype Configuration

The plugin shall use Neovim's filetype mechanism to
determine eligibility.

Example:

[source,lua]
----
require("foundation-review").setup({
  language = "en-GB",

  ollama = {
    url = "http://127.0.0.1:11434",
    model = "gemma4:12b-it-qat",
    timeout_ms = 120000,
    temperature = 0,
  },

  filetypes = {
    asciidoc = true,
    markdown = true,
    text = true,
    typst = true,
    gitcommit = true,
  },

  checks = {
    spelling = true,
    grammar = true,
    punctuation = true,
    sentence_readability = true,
  },

  quickfix = {
    auto_open = true,
  },

  keymaps = {
    review = "<leader>ar",
  },
})
----

The filetype list is an allowlist.

Unsupported filetypes shall not be reviewed by default.

=== Format Awareness

Filetype-specific adapters shall identify prose and
protect non-prose content.

Examples include:

* AsciiDoc attributes and source blocks.
* Markdown fenced code and frontmatter.
* Typst expressions and code.
* Git commit metadata.
* URLs, references and document directives.

The plugin shall not assume that all text in a buffer
is natural-language prose.

== Review Principles

=== Non-Destructive Operation

The reviewer shall never modify the buffer automatically.

All findings remain advisory.

=== British English

British English shall be the default language convention.

Valid British spelling, punctuation and grammatical
constructions shall not be flagged merely because they
differ from American English.

British collective-noun agreement shall be treated
according to context rather than rejected categorically.

=== Authorial Voice

The model shall preserve the author's meaning, tone,
terminology and intended style.

Sentence rewrites shall make only the changes needed
to improve construction and readability.

=== Local Privacy

Document content shall be sent only to the configured
local Ollama endpoint during initial operation.

No automatic cloud fallback shall be permitted.

Document contents shall not be written to diagnostic
logs by default.

=== Complementary Language Tools

Foundation Review complements Harper LSP and similar
language tools.

Harper provides continuous language diagnostics.

Foundation Review provides deliberate, context-sensitive
proofreading and sentence restructuring.

The plugin shall not require Harper to be installed.

=== Manual Invocation

Review shall occur only following an explicit command.

No model inference shall be triggered automatically
by ordinary editing, buffer changes or saves.

== Non-Functional Requirements

=== Performance

* Neovim shall remain responsive during review.
* Model execution shall be asynchronous.
* Large documents shall be segmented.
* Review cancellation shall be supported.
* Excessive concurrent model requests shall be avoided.
* A general review shall use one inference pass per chunk
  unless evaluation justifies additional passes.

=== Reliability

* Invalid model output shall not crash Neovim.
* Invalid source locations shall be rejected.
* Results shall be checked against the buffer snapshot.
* Model unavailability shall produce actionable errors.
* Partial review failures shall be reported.
* Rejected findings shall be counted and reported.
* No findings shall be fabricated.

=== Security

* The plugin shall connect only to a configured
  loopback Ollama endpoint in the initial release.
* Document text shall be treated as untrusted input.
* Model output shall never be executed as code.
* No automatic cloud fallback shall be permitted.
* No persistent plaintext temporary files shall be required.

=== Maintainability

* The implementation shall remain Lua-only.
* Neovim-native APIs shall be preferred.
* Ollama integration shall be isolated in one module.
* Filetype adapters shall be independently testable.
* Third-party runtime dependencies shall be minimised.
* Additional editorial categories shall not be introduced
  without an explicit scope decision.

== Success Criteria

The proof of concept succeeds when:

. Ollama and the configured model can be verified.
. A short document produces valid structured findings.
. British English spelling is handled correctly.
. Representative grammar errors are identified.
. Representative punctuation errors are identified.
. Unwieldy sentences receive useful advisory rewrites.
. Correct British English produces acceptably few
  false-positive findings.
. A supported Neovim buffer can be reviewed.
. Unsaved changes are included in the review.
. Findings appear in the quickfix list.
. Navigation reaches verified source locations.
. Protected markup is not reported as prose.
. Unsupported filetypes are rejected.
. No document content is modified automatically.
. The workflow functions without external connectivity.

== Model Evaluation

The initial model selection is provisional.

The existing local comparison demonstrates that
`gemma4:12b-it-qat` is a promising candidate for
structured language review.

However, the comparison used one passage and three
prompt variants, with substantial emphasis on editorial
capabilities outside the revised project scope.

The final selection shall use a proofreading-specific
corpus of approximately 40 passages.

The corpus shall include:

* Spelling errors.
* Grammar errors.
* Punctuation errors.
* Unwieldy sentences.
* Correct British English.
* Clean passages containing no errors.
* AsciiDoc markup and protected regions.
* Repeated source phrases.
* Technical prose.

Evaluation shall measure:

* Precision and false-positive rate.
* Recall by supported category.
* Correction quality.
* Sentence-rewrite usefulness.
* Exact-source quotation accuracy.
* Finding granularity.
* Markup protection.
* Review latency.

Model fit and parameter count alone shall not determine
the selection.

== Future Enhancements

Potential later capabilities include:

* Project-specific terminology dictionaries.
* Optional inline diagnostics.
* Selective correction application.
* Incremental review of changed sections.
* Review of multiple buffers.
* Configurable severity thresholds.

These are not requirements for the initial release.

General editorial analysis remains explicitly outside
the agreed scope unless separately authorised.
